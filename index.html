Thank you for participating in this survey. We are developing a new health platform that uses epigenetic profiling to monitor biological aging, disease risk, and individual health trajectories.

What is Epigenetic Profiling?

Your epigenome is influenced by lifestyle, environment, and aging. It provides a dynamic snapshot of your health at a molecular level. By analyzing your epigenetic profiling results, we aim to provide actionable insights beyond what your chronological age (birthday) can tell you.

Platform Capabilities:

  • Monitor Biological Aging: Understand if your body is aging faster or slower than your chronological age.
  • Assess Disease Risk: Identify risks for conditions like cardiovascular disease and other age-related illnesses.
  • Track Health Trajectories: Map out potential future health paths based on your current epigenetic data.
  • Provide Actionable Insights: Receive data-driven recommendations to optimize your health and longevity.
